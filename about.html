About

TIMESCAPING

Timescaping is the practice of imagining aspects of society 100 years from now, together, in live sessions, and in stories and objects from 2126.

THE PERSON BEHIND IT

CRISTIAN
BOSCHERI

Cristian Boscheri started as an engineer because he believed the world could be built differently. He studied Sustainable Energy Technology at TU Eindhoven, then spent nearly a decade co-directing wedowe, a nonprofit he co-founded, building communities and creating spaces where people could connect, think, and imagine in ways they hadn't before.

In parallel, he spent twenty years as a performer, learning how to hold a room, read an audience, and take people somewhere unexpected, while developing his own artistic practice, in which each work begins with a question and the form grows from it.

In 2024, after stepping down from the nonprofit, he felt a growing sense of overwhelm at the accelerating pace of socio-technical change. Timescaping became his way of channelling it, and the place where his artistic, technical and entrepreneurial backgrounds converge. It gives people a different way to meet. Around a scenario or a story set 100 years ahead, they learn how others think, make sense of the present together, and get more comfortable with not knowing. And there is the pleasure of imagining, with nuance and a taste for the unexpected, worlds that don't exist yet, but one day might.

The more he develops Timescaping, in exchange with participants, artists, futurists and professionals, the more aware he becomes of power, bias and the many sides of technology, AI included.

Today the practice lives in live sessions, in more than a hundred scenario cards, and in stories and artefacts from 2126.

Based in Rotterdam · Sessions across the Netherlands · Available abroad on request

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN PEOPLE IMAGINE TOGETHER

TWO PEOPLE READ THE
SAME TIMESCAPE CARD.
THEY DO NOT REACT TO
IT THE SAME WAY.

What we imagine reveals who we are now. What we fear. What we value. What we cannot yet conceive. Most people, asked to picture the future, land on doom or utopia. Timescaping opens the space in between.

People discover assumptions they didn't know they were making. They find unexpected common ground with someone who sees it completely differently. They leave looking at the present differently.

SOME OF THE THEMES EXPLORED:
AMORTALITY POST-WORK SOCIETY LIVING WITH DIFFERENCE COMMUNITY CLIMATE RESTORATION NEW SOCIAL CONTRACTS AI AND POWER

And many others, across technology, society, relationships, and what it means to be human.
