About

TIMESCAPING

Timescaping is the practice of imagining aspects of society a hundred years from now.

The way we imagine the future influences how we act today. And a society that can imagine many futures is harder to talk into just one. That matters more now that socio-technical change keeps speeding up.

Timescaping takes shape in live sessions where participants step into 2126 with detailed scenario cards, and in a growing artistic practice of stories, artefacts and other creations by Cristian.

It started in 2024 in Rotterdam, with Cristian Boscheri, artist and performer. Since then there have been sessions in cafés, theatres, universities and organisations across the Netherlands and in Italy.

The aim is to learn to sit in uncertainty together, and stay curious about each other while we do.

THE PERSON BEHIND IT

CRISTIAN
BOSCHERI

Cristian Boscheri started as an engineer because he believed the world could be built differently. He studied Sustainable Energy Technology at TU Eindhoven, then spent nearly a decade co-directing wedowe, a nonprofit he co-founded, building communities and creating spaces where people could connect, think, and imagine in ways they hadn’t before.

In parallel, he spent twenty years as an artist and performer, learning how to hold a room, read an audience, and take people somewhere unexpected, while developing his own artistic practice.

Timescaping brings these together, an appetite for what is new, the urge to question systems and ways of thinking, and the wish to bring people together.

Based in Rotterdam · Sessions across the Netherlands · Available abroad on request
